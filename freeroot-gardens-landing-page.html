Why FreeRoot?

Good food should be easier to grow.

Not everyone has the tools, materials, or experience to start a garden. FreeRoot Gardens is a community idea built around sharing those resources.

  • ✓Free raised-bed construction for selected community projects
  • ✓Reuse materials where they’re safe and suitable
  • ✓Help beginners get started growing at home
